Giovanni Leopizzi

I am currently applying to PhD programs in Finance and Economics.

I'm currently a Junior Research Scientist at New York University's Stern School of Business, supervised by Rob Richmond and Toomas Laarits.

I received my B.Sc. in Economics and Finance from Bocconi University in Milan, Italy, and my M.Sc. in Finance and Economics from the London School of Economics in London, UK.

My research interests are in empirical asset pricing - what drives asset prices and how different types of investors shape price dynamics. I'm also interested in how investors respond to shocks outside finance, with a particular focus on geopolitical events.

In my free time, I enjoy European football, tennis, and trying new places to eat around NYC.
