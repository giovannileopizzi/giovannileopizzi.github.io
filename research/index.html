The Price of Sustainability: Revealed ESG Preferences

Undergraduate thesis

Read abstract

I use the algebra of the ESG-efficient frontier to construct an index of revealed ESG taste, in units of forgone Sharpe ratio, from prices and scores alone. Estimated on ESG-sorted portfolios of the S&P500, the STOXX Europe600 and the Nikkei225 over 2011-2024, the measure is indistinguishable from zero in the full sample and moves enough on rolling windows to change sign: it rises through 2021 in the United States and Europe and turns negative by 2024. Sorting within industry as well as within market leaves the European series unchanged and reverses the American one. What the United States priced was an industry position. Only in Europe, and only until 2021, was sustainability itself priced. The series co-moves strongly with flows into sustainable funds.
